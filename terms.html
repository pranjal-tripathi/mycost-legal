What MyCost does

MyCost reads prices with your iPhone's camera and converts them into your home currency using published exchange rates. It is a convenience tool for estimating costs.

Exchange rates are estimates

  • Rates are mid-market reference rates from public sources, refreshed about once a day, and can be out of date, especially offline.
  • The rate your bank, card or a shop actually charges is usually different and may include fees. The optional card fee setting is an estimate.
  • MyCost is not financial advice. Check the amount with your payment provider before relying on it.

Price detection can be wrong

Reading prices from a camera is automatic and can pick the wrong number or the wrong currency. Always check the detected price and currency on screen; you can correct both, or force a currency, in the app.
