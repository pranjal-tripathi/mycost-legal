Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

MyCost is a price scanner and currency converter for iPhone. It is built to work on your phone, without an account, and without collecting data about you.

  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. We don't collect, sell or share personal data.
  • Camera images are read on your iPhone and never uploaded.
  • What you save stays on your iPhone. We have no server that stores it.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Camera

When you scan, MyCost reads prices from the camera image on your iPhone using Apple's on-device text recognition. Camera images are not uploaded or stored, except that when you save a price, a small cropped thumbnail of it is kept on your iPhone with that saved price.

Prices, trips and history

Saved prices (amount, currencies, rate, name, category, place and thumbnail), trips, budgets and settings are stored on your iPhone only, shared between the app and its widgets. They are not sent to us. Like other app data, they are included in your iPhone backups if you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, which Apple or you control.

Export to CSV happens only when you choose it, and the file goes only where you send it.

Location

Location is optional. If you turn on Save Location and allow access while using the app, MyCost reads your location only when you save a price and stores the coordinates with that price on your iPhone. To show a place name, the coordinates are sent to Apple's Maps service, under Apple's Privacy Policy. Opening a saved place shows it in Apple Maps.

To suggest the currency where you are, MyCost uses your iPhone's time zone and region settings, on the device. It does not use GPS for this.

Exchange rates

MyCost downloads a public exchange-rate file about once a day, and when you refresh. The file is hosted on GitHub Pages. The request carries no personal data, but like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and standard request details; see GitHub's Privacy Statement. You can limit downloads to Wi-Fi in Settings.

Purchases

MyCost Pro, Trip Passes and the free trial are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app checks what you have bought on your iPhone. The start date of the free trial is kept in your iPhone's Keychain so the trial can't restart.

Siri, Shortcuts and widgets

Widgets, controls, Shortcuts and Siri requests are handled on your iPhone using the same on-device data. Siri itself is provided by Apple under Apple's privacy terms.

Deleting your data

Delete saved prices one by one or all at once in History, and trips in Trips. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone, except the trial start date in the Keychain.

Children

MyCost is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
